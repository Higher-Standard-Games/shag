# v0.9.30 combined alpha cutoff and blending

## Cause and implementation

wfdm11's `textures/wsw_city1/cables1`, `cables2` and `wires1` request
`rgbGen vertex`, `alphaFunc GT0`, `blendFunc blend` and `depthWrite` together.
They do not request `alphaGen vertex`. Their BSP RGB channels are present and
include baked dark/black vertices. Texture alpha is intact, including partial
coverage along cable edges.

The BSP material setup and its texture-binding step previously selected
alpha-test-only whenever a cutoff existed, overriding the authored blend.
That made faint surviving pixels fully opaque, exposing solid dark fringes
and texture compression noise. The MD3 loader had the same precedence rule.

A shared `configureQ3Alpha` helper treats testing and blending independently.
Both loaders retain the cutoff and blend mode together; cutout-only stages
stay test-only. Explicit `depthWrite` sets Babylon's `forceDepthWrite` for the
transparent queue. Surviving alpha is unchanged, and RGB/alpha vertex channel
selection stays independent. This changes renderer interpretation, not map or
texture assets, and adds no new render target or pass.

## Assumptions and limits

The reported surfaces are the indexed wfdm11 cables and wires above. Their
original shader definitions match runtime metadata. Black vertex colours in
solid cable pixels remain authored lighting; this fix removes opaque fringes
rather than globally brightening cables. Matched-camera Qfusion image parity,
live multiplayer and broad transparent-surface sorting across other maps were
not verified. Generated PBR cutout materials remain outside this Standard
material path; existing builder rules keep alpha-cutoff stages on Standard.
