# Cartoon hit sprite glow

The cartoon sprite used a colored diffuse texture and white emissive color,
without an emissive texture. Babylon's GlowLayer samples emissive color and
emissive texture separately from diffuse RGB. It therefore added white across
the opaque artwork, washing out the letters and colors at strong glow settings.

`src/games/fps/client/CartoonHitEffect.ts` now assigns the selected sprite texture
to both diffuseTexture and emissiveTexture whenever a pooled sprite is spawned.
This retains texture color and alpha in glow extraction, including after reuse.
Explosion materials and global bloom/glow settings are unchanged.

GPU comparison: headless Edge, WebGL2, NVIDIA RTX 4070 SUPER. A fixture spawned
the real CartoonHitEffect with damage 100, a fixed camera and GlowLayer intensity
1.2 / blur kernel 16. Within the same scene, clearing emissiveTexture reproduced
a white silhouette; restoring it retained the BOOM lettering and pink/yellow
artwork. The texture was ready and the material rendered in both captures.
Browser console and failed-request lists were empty. This is a focused glow
reproducer, not a complete highest-profile in-match test.

A second comparison used the Pretty profile's actual glow intensity, 0.35,
verified in SettingsModel.ts. The fixed POW artwork retained clear lettering
and saturated colors. Both comparisons used a continuous engine render loop;
an earlier fixture without that loop produced blank captures and was discarded.
