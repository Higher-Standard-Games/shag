# Alpha.2 bot perception, navigation and ledge boosts

Status: validated for the v1.0.0-alpha.2 bot hotfix release.
The observations below describe pre-release local validation.
Latest frozen-source gates pass (5,541 tests, one skipped); see the follow-up
validation at the end. Initial failed runs below are retained as investigation history.

## Scope and assumptions

The reported failures were Duel on DM2 and DM5 with automatic bot difficulty.
Validation uses the installed `washoi_dm2` and `washoi_dm5`, default 125 Hz FPS
movement, and Hard bots. Smooth view control remains in `BotAimController`.
There are no protocol, ranking schema, or movement-rule changes.

## Findings and changes

- Fresh audible tracks had age zero and `_getActiveTrackedEnemies` skipped them.
  Repeated footsteps refreshed the track every snapshot, suppressing chase
  selection continuously. Age zero is now valid. Memory and extrapolation also
  used 60 Hz constants against a 125 Hz server; they now use the physics clock.
- Hearing an enemy outside the view cone did not request a visual check. Heard
  enemies now get a line-of-sight check, with the existing notice delay and wall
  occlusion. This does not expose silent, unseen opponents.
- Blocked item routes returned stationary input. Failed approaches now back away,
  cool down the edge/pickup and replan. Two seconds without position progress
  also triggers recovery, including a low-vertical-speed airborne hang; crouch
  releases a ledge grab. Mandatory objective holds retain their behavior.
- Duel territory selection scores reachable floor/spawn nodes by height, recent
  enemy sightlines and travel cost. It avoids recent destinations and routes
  requiring a deep drop. Healthy bots with a ranged weapon patrol upper floors
  instead of descending for lower items; low-health resupply still wins. Combat
  and chase movement probe floor support to retain height against an enemy below.
  Timed-item arrivals patrol nearby rather than watch from a stationary spot.
- Automatic lobby invitations have a Hard floor and can select Insane from the
  rating match. Explicit developer preset requests still support all levels.
- Chase already had slide and strafe-jump sequences. Fixing chase selection makes
  these available to continuously heard targets; integration tests cover both.

## Slide to ledge to boosted jump

`LedgeSlideJump` considers existing jump/fall route edges for sufficiently skilled
bots. It requires grounded running speed, aligned momentum, enough distance to
establish a slide, an expired slide cooldown and no firing command.

The planner samples the approaching ledge and simulates up to 150 movement ticks
with the FPS `MovementProfile` and collision world. It crouchslides, then sends a
jump before crossing the edge, using the existing slide-jump speed boost. It
requires a supported landing near the route destination and rejects lava/slime.
The sequence sends the proven per-tick script once, keeps the view steady and
checks the live landing; failed traversal enters normal recovery.

The synthetic collision fixture has a platform ending at x=180 and another
starting at x=360. Starting at x=0 with 420 units/s, the proof jumps at tick 37,
reaches about 544 units/s at takeoff and lands near x=506. Tests also reject a
missing platform, hazardous landing, sideways momentum and insufficient run-up.
This is conservative, opportunistic execution of existing routes, not a new
route generator. No ledge-boost sequence appeared in the short real-map samples.
Custom server movement settings and latency-induced start-state differences have
not been calibrated; landing verification and recovery are still necessary.

## Follow-up: running, purposeful jumps and a small Duel lead handicap

The next playtest found excessive hopping and slow approaches despite improved
aggression. This follow-up keeps the perception/high-ground work and tunes the
movement defaults:

- `_moveToward`, precise navigation, recovery, teleporter/jumppad approaches and
  post-slide pickup movement request Forward + Sprint. Urgent travel and wall
  feelers no longer add lateral buttons merely for variety: the FPS profile
  explicitly caps diagonal/sideways input at walk speed even with Sprint held.
- Random patrol/travel jumps, blind wall-feeler jumps, unconditional DODGE-state
  jumps and distance-only combat hops are removed. Existing required jump routes,
  learned wall jumps and proven ledge boosts remain available.
- Optional route strafe jumps require at least the default 400 ups running speed
  in both the brain and sequence gates. Crouchslide selection runs first. Slow
  bots accelerate on the ground instead of hopping to build their initial speed.
- Combat threats can request a dodge jump below running speed: that is a reasoned
  dodge, not routine travel. A dodge jump is issued for one decision, always with
  sideways movement, and at most once per second. The bot continues dodging on
  the ground between pulses. A slide that falls below running speed cannot
  jump-cancel, including when it is stuck against geometry.
- Recovery, interruption and sequence completion explicitly cancel the client's
  old per-tick movement script before applying the next decision. Previously,
  the brain could abandon a route while the client continued transmitting its
  old jump commands. A replacement script can start in the same decision.
- Duel with exactly two roster players gets a fixed +40 ms reaction-time setting
  when the bot leads by more than two frags. The adjustment applies to perception
  notice, large view turns and projectile notice, and is removed at a lead of
  two or less. It is recomputed from scores rather than accumulated, leaves aim
  accuracy alone, and does not change ratings or stored difficulty presets.
  The local score map is refreshed each snapshot so departed opponents cannot
  suppress this calculation.

Running is subject to the same rules as humans: firing, ADS, crouching and combat
strafing can prevent sprint speed. Authored movement scripts keep the exact inputs
that were validated offline; objective planting/defusing still needs a hold.
These are not overrides of the shared movement rules.
