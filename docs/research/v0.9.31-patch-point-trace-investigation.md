# Point traces on round patch caps and seams

Observed 2026-10-17 while validating SHAGGED round presets. This is a separate
runtime collision finding, not a remaining round-preset API requirement.

## Reproduction and evidence

The scene in `src/engine/editor/server/__tests__/RoundPatchCompile.test.ts`
compiles a capped cylinder, tube, cone and planar disk through MakeBSP/MakeLight.
It is enclosed in a structural room. All surfaces use `blx/blx_grid`, compiled
with SOLID contents. The native export format has been corrected and the BSP
contains the expected finite patch controls and upward cap normals.

With `SweepCollisionBackend.trace`, Babylon-space point sweeps
`{radius: 0, height: 0}` from height 160 to 16 gave these observed results:

- Cylinder center `(x=0, z=0)` missed its top at height 96. A noncentral
  `(x=8, z=3)` point hit it.
- Tube annular top at `(x=-100, z=3)` missed height 96.
- Disk center `(x=0, z=-128)` missed height 32.
- The cylinder-side point sweep from `(-80,48,0)` to `(80,48,0)` missed the
  expected contact at X=-48.
- A point sweep onto the cone at X=136 hit the sloped surface near height 72.

Finite-size sweeps `{radius: 1, height: 2}` hit the cap/side fixtures. The final
native regression verifies those contacts, the cone slope and passage through
the tube's hollow center. The small hull has the engine's asymmetric vertical
extent (-0.75/+1.25), which is included in expected contact heights.

Temporary diagnostic runs disabled facet spatial indexes and then visited all
BSP leaves. The cylinder point miss persisted. Cap faces had nonzero leaf
references and generated facets. This narrows the investigation toward facet
contact geometry or floating-point edge handling; it does not establish the
root cause. Temporary overrides/logging were removed from the final test.
Diagnostic output remains in `.cache/shagged-round-debug.log` on the test machine.

## Resolution (2026-10-17)

Three independently relevant numerical/geometry problems were confirmed:

1. Axial and edge bevel distances bounded only the front triangle. An axial
   plane could therefore cut away the intended 0.5-unit backing thickness.
   Point sweeps then had equal entry and exit fractions and failed the strict
   interval test. Supporting distances now enclose the full extruded triangle.
2. Summing nine weighted control positions introduced tiny height differences
   into constant-height caps (96 became 96.00000000000003). A point through the
   cap's collapsed center encountered near-parallel border planes with spurious
   exit fractions around negative 26 billion. de Casteljau interpolation keeps
   constant coordinates and control endpoints exact, removing those tilted
   planes without changing the quadratic surface or tessellation density.
3. Float32 packing independently rounded neighboring facet borders. A rotated
   flat patch at map offset 8192 still missed a point at local (12.8,-12.8).
   Packed facet planes now use Float64Array. Reverting only that storage to
   Float32 reproduced failures on rotated/translated seams after the geometry
   fixes. This doubles packed plane storage from 16 to 32 bytes per plane;
   brush storage and the hot trace arithmetic are unchanged.

No global tolerance, trace clip epsilon, BSP traversal or PVS behavior changed.
An experimental distance-snapping branch was removed after tests established
that geometry construction and precision were sufficient for these cases.

## Regression evidence and limits

- The new regressions fail against the previous physics implementation.
- Facet geometry checks require all back vertices to remain inside every plane.
- Tessellation checks preserve constant coordinates and endpoints on all axes.
- Synthetic collision tests cover flat/sloped patches, three rotations and
  map offsets 0/+8192/-8192, with interior, shared-edge, boundary and outside
  point traces. Existing small/player-hull and embedded-contact tests remain.
- Native MakeBSP/MakeLight round-preset tests verify the original cylinder,
  tube, disk and cylinder-side point misses, small hull contacts, the cone
  slope, and intentional misses through the tube center and outside cylinder.
- The recorded wfdm11 route benchmark retained identical trace/movement results
  for all 580 samples. Baseline/candidate median query-batch times were both
  0.0077 ms; p95 both 0.0105 ms. These are one Node run each, not a browser FPS
  measurement or proof of unchanged cost on every map. Artifacts:
  `.cache/patch-point-baseline.json`, `.cache/patch-point-candidate.json`.
- The native test requires available compilers; they were present on this
  machine. No interactive in-game QA has been performed for this checkpoint.
