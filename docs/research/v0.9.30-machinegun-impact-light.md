## Adjustment

`HitscanEffects.fireTracer`, used for local and remote machinegun shots, now
passes a peak impact-light intensity of 0.25 instead of the shared helper's 25.
The helper accepts this internal per-call intensity; other hit-effect call sites
retain their existing values. The warm colour, 60-unit range, 0.16-second effect
lifetime, streak, sparks, wall mark, fade and pooled-light ownership are unchanged.
No gameplay damage, hit detection or networking behavior changes.

The legacy bullet peak was much stronger than the corrected rocket flash,
whose default peak is 0.1 times scale 13, or 1.3. The new bullet peak is intended
to provide a small local highlight while sparks carry the hit feedback.

Real-GPU fixture: `node .cache/mg-light-controlled.mjs`, served by
`node .claude/skills/browser-probe/scripts/private-vite.mjs 3100`.
It invokes the actual tracer-impact path against a frozen BSP-style material,
uses the actual point-light pool/cluster, holds the light at its peak for stable
measurement and compares no light, legacy 25, tuned 0.25, then no light again.
Spark and wall-mark creation are suppressed only inside the scratch fixture to
isolate the dynamic light. Both WebGL2 and WebGPU produce identical results:

| State | Centre RGB | Yellow/white clipped pixels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No flash | 113, 71, 42 | 0 |
| Legacy peak 25 | 255, 255, 83 | 4 |
| Tuned peak 0.25 | 116, 72, 43 | 0 |
| Released flash | 113, 71, 42 | 0 |

The tuned flash remains above the unlit baseline, avoids the previous clipping,
and returns its pool light at intensity zero when the effect clears. No GPU
errors or HTTP failures occurred in this controlled fixture. Logs/results:
`.cache/mg-light-controlled.log` and `.cache/mg-light-controlled.json`.

An earlier wfdm11 probe encountered a browser `ERR_NO_BUFFER_SPACE` while loading
an optional KTX2 decoder, causing source-texture fallbacks. Its first capture
also preceded material readiness. Those map-frame measurements were excluded;
the isolated fixture above provides a stable before/after comparison.

Assumption: the reported glow is the machinegun tracer impact, as indicated by
the shot path and screenshot. Live combat with repeated impacts, matched-camera
wfdm11 screenshot parity and the final subjective brightness balance remain for
play testing. The change is a local tuning adjustment and adds no new resources
or player-facing setting. Final subjective brightness remains open to play testing.
