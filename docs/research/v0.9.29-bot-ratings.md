# Bot games are rated: bots as Private-board accounts

**Status:** all four steps done on `v0.9.29-demo-player`.

## The rule we want

- **Public** matches are human only, and rated on the Public board.
- **Private** matches may have bots (only private rooms can invite them), and
  they are rated too, on the Private board, separate from Public.

So a player who only ever plays bots still has a rating, a rank and a board to
climb.

## What happens now

A private game against a bot is stored, counted for wins and losses, and never
rated. Seen on 2026-10-13 with Player_1086's two Duels against a medium bot:
both are in `ranking_results` as `recorded / eligible`, with the rating
`pending: bot_reference_pending`. Every one of the 9 bot games in
`server-data/players.db` has that status, and the account has no `ratings` row.

Three things keep the player and the bots off every board:

| # | Cause | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The skill board starts from `ratings`, so an account with records and no rating isn't a row. | `SKILL_RANKED` in `src/lobby/MatchResultStore.ts` |
| 2 | A bot game rates only against a *calibrated fixed reference*: a reviewed artifact per bot configuration × mode × map, bound to the bot build, model and map bytes, made from about 30 recorded trials against at least 3 established humans (`ranking:pilot`, `ranking:references`). None is installed (`bot_calibrations` is empty), so everything waits. | `botMatchRatingGroups` in `src/shared/ranking/BotMatchRatingGroups.ts`, `BotCalibrationStore`, `BotPilotStore` |
| 3 | Bots have no accounts, so they can never be rows. | by design today |

Point 2 is the real block. Its gate almost never opens for a small player
base, and any change to bot code (the behavior digest) invalidates a reference
once installed.

## Proposal: each bot preset is an account

One account per preset: **Bot · Easy**, **Bot · Medium**, **Bot · Hard**,
**Bot · Insane**. Each has a rating in each mode's Private pool, as a player
does, and updates from every eligible private game it plays, exactly like a
human.

- **No calibration step.** A bot account starts where a new player starts
  (μ 25, σ 8.33) and settles from real games. TrueSkill already weights a
  result by both sides' uncertainty, so the first games against an unsettled
  bot move a human less than later ones.
- **Farming limits itself.** A bot that loses often drops, and beating a low
  bot earns almost nothing. Bots never play Public, so the Public board can't
  be farmed at all.
- **The bots are on the board**, as rows marked as bots.

### Why a bot's strength stays fixed within a match

A rating is a measure against a known opponent. A bot that turns itself up
mid-match while losing has no single strength: its account would be rated as
an average of several bots, and a win against it would mean different things
depending on when it changed. That is the existing `bot_reference_changed`
exclusion (the bot server can change a bot's difficulty live), and it stays.

Changing *between* matches is the right place for "it gets harder as I get
better": the room fields the preset whose rating is nearest the player's own.
Each preset stays its own account with its own honest rating. That is a later
step (below); a fixed preset choice works first.

### Details

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| One bot of a preset in the match | The bot account is rated and updated like any player. |
| Two or more bots of one preset (FFA against three Mediums) | One account can't take two seats in one TrueSkill game. Each copy plays as a **fixed** copy of that account's current rating; humans update, that bot account does not from this match. `rateWithFixedReferences` already does exactly this for the calibrated references. |
| A preset bot changed mid-match | Excluded, `bot_reference_changed`, as now. |
| A custom difficulty (bot-server control API only, not the lobby's invite) | Excluded: `bot_custom`. It names no account. |
| Late join, team change, interruption, forfeit rules | Unchanged: the human rules apply to bot seats too. |
| Bot accounts and login | No key: `key_hash` is a hash of random bytes nobody holds. The four names join the reserved names, as the bot server's already are. A small `bot_accounts(preset, account_id)` table says which accounts are bots; `players` keeps its schema. |

### The board

- Private shows bot rows with a bot mark next to the name.
- Players with records and no rating yet are listed below the rated rows as
  *unrated*, with their W/L/D, on both boards, so nobody who has played is
  missing. (After the change, that is only someone whose every game was
  excluded.)
- Public is unchanged.

### Existing results

Ratings are derived from the stored snapshots, so no new rating policy was
needed: the numbers (`shag-trueskill-v1`) are unchanged, only who is rated.
Ranking schema 3 adds `bot_accounts`; opening a schema 1 or 2 store rates
every stored result again in sequence order, adds the bot accounts' records,
and drops `bot_calibrations`. On a copy of this machine's `server-data` (19
rated results, 2026-10-13): the 9 pending bot games became rated, Public
pools came out unchanged, the replay check passed, and the Private Duel board
reads Bot · Medium 1698 (6–1), Player_1086 1571 (1–1), Bot · Easy 1469 (1–0),
StatsTest100 1388 (2–1), and the rest.

## What goes away (done)

Under the no-two-ways rule the calibration path is removed rather than kept
beside the accounts:

- `bot_calibrations`, `BotCalibrationStore`, `BotPilotStore`,
  `BotRatingReferences`, the `ranking:pilot` and `ranking:references` CLIs.
- The `pending / bot_reference_pending` rating status. A bot game is applied
  or excluded, like any other.
- The calibration and pilot sections of `lobby-server.md` and the pilot
  workflow in `ranking_matches.md`.

`BotReference` stays: it is how a mid-match change and a custom bot are
detected. `FixedReferenceRating` stays for the duplicate-preset case.

## Steps

1. **Bot accounts and rating** (done). `bot_accounts`, created on first use;
   private bot matches map preset seats to accounts and go through the human
   eligibility path; duplicate presets play fixed; custom bots excluded.
   Schema 3 and the re-rate on opening an older store.
2. **Board** (done). A row whose account is a bot preset's carries `bot`
   from the lobby and a cyan BOT tag on the podium and in the rows. The
   pending count is gone from the protocol (`localProcessing`, the stats
   summary), the Profile and the board's wording. Unrated rows were
   declined: with bot accounts the only players with records and no rating
   are those whose every game was excluded, the board already says "N of
   your matches did not qualify", and unranked rows would break the
   podium, the "points behind #n" step and the rail for that rare case.
3. **Remove** the calibration path and its docs (done, with step 1: the
   rating function's signature changed under it).
4. **Auto difficulty** (done). The room's **+ Add bot** never sent a level, so
   every bot was Medium; an invite without one now fields the preset whose
   Private rating in that mode is nearest the inviter's (`chooseBotPreset`,
   `MatchResultStore.botPresetFor`). A player with no rating meets Medium.
   A preset with no rating yet is placed by its order, a starting sigma
   apart around a new player's mu (Easy 16.7, Medium 25, Hard 33.3, Insane
   41.7) — only for this choice, never as a rating — so Hard and Insane get
   played, and rated, as players climb. The level never changes inside a
   match. The reply names the preset, and the room shows each bot's level
   on its seat (`LobbyPlayerInfo.botLevel`, from the bot's reference).
   An explicit level still works for the API and the console.

Gates for 1–3: typecheck, `npm run test:run`, `npm run ranking:acceptance`,
build, Biome; a localhost private Duel against a bot that moves both ratings
and shows both rows; a lobby restart that replays the stored results into v2.

## Decided

- The board names a bot by what it is and its level: **Bot · Medium**. The
  name it played under in the match (Ramen, Gyoza, …) stays on the scoreboard
  and in the demo; the account is the preset.
