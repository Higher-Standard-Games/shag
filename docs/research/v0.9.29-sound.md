# v0.9.29: sound tuning

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-13. **Source:** engine `edc4b2bb`, FPS `58c3600`.
**Status:** SND01–SND12 done on `v0.9.29-sound-tuning`. Media is out of
scope: the shipped sounds are being replaced along with the new maps.

Two halves: how the client plays sound, and which match moments have a sound.
Warsow's behaviour is taken from the qfusion source (`gameshared`, `cgame`) and
the gametype scripts in `data0_21pure.pk3` (`progs/gametypes/**/*.as`).

## The complaint: an enemy's shot sounds like your own

It is the distance curve. Three things add up:

1. **Our falloff is linear and long.** Remote weapon fire uses the default
   96–2400 linear range. Warsow uses an inverse curve with a 125-unit
   reference distance, which halves the gain at twice the reference distance and
   keeps a long quiet tail:

   | Distance | SHAG now (linear 96–2400) | Warsow `ATTN_NORM` | Warsow `ATTN_DISTANT` |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 125 | 0.99 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
   | 250 | 0.93 | 0.50 | 0.67 |
   | 500 | 0.82 | 0.25 | 0.40 |
   | 1000 | 0.61 | 0.13 | 0.22 |
   | 2000 | 0.17 | 0.06 | 0.12 |

   An enemy 500 units away plays at 0.82 of full; with the 0.95 call volume
   that is 0.78, against 0.9 for your own shot. In Warsow the same enemy is
   at a quarter.
2. **Remote fire is mixed slightly louder than your own** (0.95 against 0.9).
3. **The listener can be stale.** It attaches to `scene.activeCamera` only when
   a 3D sound plays. Measured in the browser: before any 3D sound, the listener
   was still on a disposed weapon camera, detached, at a position hundreds of
   units from the view. The first sounds after a camera change (respawn,
   spectate, demo start) are placed against the old position.

Babylon also hands WebAudio a listener facing backwards (its right-handed
conversion negates the forward vector and leaves positions alone): measured
forward is the exact negation of the camera's. Left and right still come out
right, and front and back swap. Equal-power panning, the default, cannot tell
front from back, so it shows only if HRTF is turned on.

## Warsow's model

`gameshared/q_shared.h` and `q_shared.c`:

- Model 3, inverse clamped: `gain = ref / (ref + attn * (dist - ref))`, with
  `dist` clamped to `[125, 8000]`. WebAudio's `inverse` model with
  `refDistance 125` and `rolloffFactor = attn` is the same curve; it does not
  clamp at the far end, where the gain is already 0.03 or less for every class
  but `ATTN_NONE`.
- Classes (`gameshared/gs_public.h`): `ATTN_NONE 0`, `ATTN_DISTANT 0.5`,
  `ATTN_NORM 1`, `ATTN_IDLE 2.5`, `ATTN_STATIC 5`, `ATTN_WHISPER 10`.

What uses which class (`cgame/cg_events.cpp`, `cgame/cg_lents.cpp`):

| Sound | Viewer's own | Others |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon fire | global (no position) | `ATTN_NORM`; plasma `ATTN_IDLE` |
| Rocket and grenade explosions | — | `ATTN_DISTANT` |
| Plasma impact, grenade bounce, riotgun impact, item respawn | — | `ATTN_IDLE` |
| Electrobolt and instagun impact, laser hit, ricochet, movers | — | `ATTN_STATIC` |
| Blade hits | — | `ATTN_NORM` |
| Gunblade blast | — | by knockback: `ATTN_IDLE`, `ATTN_NORM`, `ATTN_DISTANT` |
| Weapon switch, empty click, respawn, teleport | global | `ATTN_NORM` (click `ATTN_IDLE`) |
| Jump, dash, walljump, fall, pain, death | the player's own attenuation | the player's own attenuation |
| Hit and kill confirmation | local, `cg_volume_hitsound` | — |
| Announcer | local, own channel, queued | — |

Mixer: `cg_volume_effects`, `cg_volume_players`, `cg_volume_hitsound`,
`cg_volume_announcer`, `cg_volume_voicechats`, all default 1.0. Announcer
events can be immediate (holding the channel 1.5 s) or queued behind it. The
server picks pain by remaining health, not by the damage taken.

## How SHAG plays sound

| Part | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Music | `AudioManager` | HTML audio, playlists, `s_volume` / `s_musicenabled`. |
| Effects | `AudioV2Sfx` | Babylon Audio V2, one `sfx` bus (`s_sfxvolume`, default 0.7, `s_sfxenabled`). |
| 2D one-shots | `playSfx2D` | One cached sound per path, `maxInstances` per call site (1–8). |
| 3D one-shots | `playSfx3D` | A new spatial sound per play from a cached buffer; linear falloff with each call site's own range; at most 48 alive, the oldest cut. |
| Loops | `playLoop3D` | Map speakers; their range is the only one derived from an attenuation. |
| Listener | `AudioV2Sfx.syncListener` | Attached to `scene.activeCamera` when a 3D sound plays. |
| Preload | `preloadFPSAudio` | Every gameplay bank at map load; movers in `BspEntityHandlers`. |
| Settings | `AudioPage` | Music and effects: on/off and one volume each. |

## Findings

| ID | Problem | Evidence | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SND01 | Distant sounds stay loud: linear falloff with hand-picked ranges (64–1250 to 128–3200) instead of attenuation classes on an inverse curve. | Table above; literals in each `FPS*Audio` file. | S |
| SND02 | The listener goes stale on camera changes and is only re-attached by the next 3D sound. | Browser probe: detached on a disposed weapon camera before the first 3D sound. | S |
| SND03 | Duel, FFA and Amphi had no announcer of their own. Warsow's `generic/matchstates.as`, `duel.as`. | M |
| SND04 | No announcer queue; lines can overlap. S&D already dropped `get_ready_to_fight` because it collided. | `ROUND` comment in `FPSBombAudio.ts`; Warsow queues on its own channel. | S |
| SND05 | No lead announcements (taken, tied, lost; team variants). Warsow plays them while the match is live. | `sounds/announcer/score/` unused; standings are in `MatchStateManager`. | S–M |
| SND06 | No ready-up nag in warmup. Decided: once a minute, to players not ready. | `pleasereadyup` unused; Warsow's `readyAnnouncementEnabled`. | S |
| SND07 | Hit confirmation is round-robin over `hit_0`–`hit_3`. Decided: by damage, 0 weakest. Team damage has no sound of its own. | `playDamageConfirmationSound` uses `chooseVariant`. | S |
| SND08 | One effects bus: the announcer and hit sounds turn down with everything else. Decided: separate volumes, Warsow's five. | `AudioV2Sfx` makes one `sfx` bus. | S–M |

| SND10 | The 48-sound cap evicts the oldest 3D sound whatever it is; a footstep can cut an explosion. | `MAX_ACTIVE_SPATIAL_SOUNDS` with `shift()`. | S |

## Match moments

| Moment | Warsow | SHAG now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warmup, not ready | `pleasereadyup` (engine side) | Nothing |
| Countdown starts | `countdown/get_ready_to_fight` | S&D's defense start line in every mode (S&D: per-side start line, by design) |
| 3, 2, 1 | `countdown/3`, `2`, `1` (engine side) | Each number once per frame outside S&D (S&D: over the round freeze) |
| Match starts | `countdown/fight` | `fight01` (S&D: each round) |
| Lead changes | `score/taken_lead`, `tied_lead`, `lost_lead`, team variants | Nothing |
| Overtime | `overtime/going_to_overtime`, `overtime`, `suddendeath` | Nothing; Duel extends on a draw silently |
| Match over | `postmatch/game_over` | Nothing |
| Round won or lost | `ctf/score_team`, `score_enemy` (CA, DA, bomb) | Nothing |

Timeouts and votes have announcer lines but are not features here.

## Proposed order

1. **SND01 + SND02:** attenuation classes on the inverse curve, Warsow's
   values; remote fire at your own shot's volume; the listener follows the view
   camera every frame. This is the distance complaint.
2. **SND07 + SND12:** hit sound by damage, pain by health.
3. **SND03 + SND04 + SND06:** an announcer with its own channel and queue,
   driven by match phases, for every mode; S&D moves onto it; the ready nag.
4. **SND08:** five buses and the Settings rows.
5. **SND05:** lead announcements.
6. **SND09 + SND10:** voice slots per player; eviction that spares long sounds.
7. **SND11:** remove the dead `sounds:` fields.

## Done

- SND01, SND02: `f9dddfcb`, fps `b3fa637`.
- SND07, and a 400 ms pain debounce per player (Warsow's `pain_debounce_time`): fps `03d3577`.
- SND03, SND04, SND06: `FPSAnnouncer`, one voice for every announcer line; S&D
  speaks through it and is the only mode that runs its round lines. A countdown
  of three seconds goes straight to the numbers ("get ready to fight" needs
  1.5 s in front of "3": Amphi's five-second countdown has it).

- SND05: lead calls from the scoreboard, team lines in team modes.
- SND08: `effects`, `players`, `hits` and `announcer` buses under the effects
  bus, `cg_volume_*` cvars, a Mix card in Settings. Warsow's fifth,
  `cg_volume_voicechats`, waits for voice chat.
- SND09: a 3D sound can take a slot (`<entity>:pain`, `<entity>:body`); the
  next sound in the slot cuts it. SND10: over the cap, the sound nearest its
  end is cut.
- SND11: the `sounds` fields are gone from `WeaponDef`, `ProjectileDef` and
  `EntityDef`, and from every definition.
- SND12: the server puts the victim's pain tier (Warsow's 20 / 35 / 60 health
  thresholds) on the damage event, wire code 25: code 23's layout and a tier
  byte. Codes 10 and 23 are unchanged, so older demos play, their pain going
  by damage.
- Countdowns are 5 seconds in every mode but S&D, as Warsow's
  `g_countdown_time`, so each match opens with "get ready to fight".

## Decided

- Hit sounds: `hit_0` weakest, `hit_3` hardest; easy to flip.
- Ready-up nag: once a minute during warmup.
- Separate volumes, as Warsow's.
- Media: out of scope here.

## Still open

- Hit-sound damage thresholds. Warsow's server code choosing the index was not
  found; the pain tiers' 25 / 50 / 75 are used.
- HRTF: it gives front and back, at a cost per voice, and needs Babylon's
  backwards listener corrected first. Worth a listening test after SND01.
