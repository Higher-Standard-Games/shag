# The highlights player: its real shape

**Status:** all five steps done on `v0.9.29-demo-player`.

Highlights (**⚡ Highlights** on a demo card, or `demoplay cinematic <minutes>`)
cut a recording into a reel of its best moments: slowed, shot from scripted
cameras, with instant replays. It is older code than the demo player work in
[v0.9.29-demo-player.md](v0.9.29-demo-player.md), and it shows.

## How it is built

| Part | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analysis | `shared/demo/DemoAnalyzer.ts` | Scores frags, multi-kills, airborne frags, streaks and movement into highlight candidates. |
| Reel | `shared/demo/SequenceBuilder.ts` | Picks the best non-overlapping candidates for the length asked, gives each a camera preset, a speed and speed keyframes, inserts 0.25× replays of the best ones, and assigns transitions. |
| Camera paths | `shared/demo/CinematicCameraPaths.ts` | Keyframed fly-by paths (orbit, dolly, under-the-jump) for the free camera; chase presets name an entity instead. |
| Player | `client/CinematicDemoPlayer.ts` | Each frame: advances segments, applies speed keyframes, moves the free camera along its path; at a segment start, seeks and switches the camera controller. |
| Overlay | `client/CinematicHUD.ts` | Its own info card and progress bar, with inline styles. |

**It is not a second demo player.** Highlights run on the same `DemoService`,
`DemoViewer`, event path, renderer and `SpectatorController` as a normal demo;
`CinematicDemoPlayer` only steers them. The duplication is in the overlay: a
second bar (`CinematicHUD`) beside `DemoViewerHUD`, which highlight mode
builds and keeps hidden.

## Findings

Measured on `wdm17_duel_20261013_121910` (a 1-minute reel, 10 segments) with
the browser probe.

| ID | Problem | Evidence | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HL01 | **The slowed "victim" shots freeze.** `victim_pov` follows the player who dies; they die early in the shot, leave the recording, and the camera holds its last pose for the rest of the slowed segment, then jumps at the next cut. This is most of the "jittery" slow motion. | At 0.5×, the chase camera stood still on 1890 of 2103 frames; the followed entity had no sample (`player_2` absent from the frames after its death). At 1×: 0 of 1277. | S |
| HL02 | **The fly-by camera steps at the recording rate.** `CinematicDemoPlayer.update` reads `currentTick`, the tick of the frame last delivered, not the smooth `playheadTick`; in slow motion each step holds for several screen frames. | At 0.25×, the free camera stood still on 1811 of 2059 frames. | S |
| HL03 | **Speed changes are steps.** Speed keyframes switch 1× → 0.25× in one frame; a slow-motion moment should ease in and out. | `SequenceBuilder._assignSpeed`, applied as-is per frame. | S |
| HL04 | **Fly-by shots miss the action.** Paths are fixed around the highlight's midpoint, ignore walls and don't follow the players, so shots look at ceilings, sit in corners, or show no one. | Screenshots: a shot looking up at the ceiling and walls; a replay orbit with only a pickup in frame. | M |
| HL05 | **The transitions are never drawn.** Every segment is given a transition (fade to black, white flash before a replay) and nothing draws one: every change is a hard cut. | `_assignTransitions` writes them; no reader. | S |
| HL06 | **Whether a shot follows the killer or the victim is a coin toss.** `peakTick % 5 === 0` picks `victim_pov`. | `SequenceBuilder._assignPreset`. | S |
| HL07 | **The overlay is the old style**: a grey info card and a thin bar with inline CSS, unlike the playback bar and the HUD. | `CinematicHUD` (304 lines). | M |
| HL08 | **There is no way out** of either player: the menu hides when playback starts and nothing brings it back. | No exit control or key; only `DemosHost.show()` on a start failure. | S |

## Proposal

1. **Exit, for both players (HL08).** A yellow **✕** plate in the top-left
   corner, in the FPS counter's style (the gold gradient, the clipped corner,
   mirrored), and **Esc**. Both stop playback and return to the Demos page
   the viewer came from.
2. **Smooth slow motion (HL01–HL03).**
   - A victim shot follows the victim up to the frag, then cuts to the killer's eyes for the rest of the segment, so a slowed shot never watches an empty space.
   - The fly-by path reads `playheadTick`.
   - Speed changes ease over a short ramp instead of stepping.
3. **One bar (HL07).** Highlights use `DemoViewerHUD` in a reel mode:
   - The timeline is the reel, with a tick per segment.
   - Play/pause, previous and next segment.
   - The segment's card (FRAG, MULTI KILL, the player, the tags) as a HUD plate in the same style.

   `CinematicHUD` is deleted.
4. **Transitions (HL05).** Draw them: a short fade to black at the start and end, a white flash into a replay. Another option is to remove them from the reel data, but the reel reads flat without them.
5. **Shots that see the action (HL04, HL06).**
   - Fly-by cameras look at the players involved, read from the interpolated samples, instead of a fixed midpoint.
   - A path is pulled in front of any wall between the camera and its subject.
   - The killer/victim choice follows a rule, not the tick number: victim view only when the victim saw it coming, say a direct hit taken while facing the killer.

Order: 1 and 2 first (small, and they fix what feels broken), then 3 and 4
(the look), then 5 (the larger one).

## Decided

- Both players share one control bar and the HUD switch. The game HUD shows
  only from a player's eyes: on a fly-by shot it stays off whatever the
  switch says, and the switch dims.
- Either player's controls minimize to a strip along the bottom, for
  recording. A demo opens with the HUD on and the bar up; a reel opens in
  movie mode, the HUD off and the bar down to a strip. A duel cup viewer
  turns the HUD on with one click.

## Done

- **Exit (HL08).** A gold ✕ plate in the top-left corner, the FPS counter's
  plate mirrored, and Esc. Leaving stops playback and the Demos page comes
  back (`DemoService.exit`, `setExitHandler` from `DemosView`).
- **Slow motion (HL01–HL03).**
  - A victim shot hands over to the killer at the frag, and either player
    gives way to the other while out of the recording.
  - The fly-by path, segment ends and speed keyframes read the playhead.
  - Speed changes ease at 12/s.
  - Measured on the same reel: the chase camera at 0.5× stood still on 2 of
    1622 frames (was 1890 of 2103); the fly-by at 0.25× on 0 of 2322 (was
    1811 of 2059).
- **One bar (HL07).**
  - `DemoViewerHUD` has a reel mode: a tick per shot, previous/next, "Shot 2/10 · 0.25x", and a click on the timeline jumps to that shot.
  - The shot card is a HUD plate.
  - Reel progress comes from the playhead within its shots instead of counted wall time, so a skip or a pause leaves it right.
  - `CinematicHUD` and the overlay controller's cinematic mode are gone.
- **Found on the way: a second map load hung at "Warming up effect
  shaders".** The generated fallback textures (`$whiteimage`, the halo
  gradient) were cached across map loads; unloading a map disposed them, the
  cache handed the disposed ones to the next map, and the material warm-up
  waited for good on materials that could never be ready. Leaving a demo and
  playing another, or any second map using them in one session, stopped
  there. The cache now hands out only live textures of the same scene, and
  the warm-up's material pass gives up after 10 s with a warning, as the
  scene-ready gate after it already does.

- **Transitions (HL05).** `CinematicDemoPlayer.getVeil()` computes, from
  the playhead and the shot's real playing time, a veil over the picture:
  from black into the first shot and to black after the last, a white flash
  rising out of a shot and falling into its replay, and a crossfade between
  two players' shots drawn as a dip through black (60 %): a true crossfade
  would need two cameras rendering at once. The veil is part of the picture,
  so `H` leaves it. The reel's first half-second plays under the loading
  screen, so the opening fade is mostly hidden.
- **Fly-bys that see the action (HL04).**
  - The path's Catmull-Rom curve is used: `interpolatePath` had blended linearly and kinked at every keyframe. Linear stays the default, so the timedemo paths are unchanged.
  - The camera looks at the killer's chest as drawn, and at the victim too while the two are within 300 units.
  - A solid surface between them and the camera (Q3 `CONTENTS_SOLID`, what stops a shot) pulls it in to 24 units in front of the wall.
  - Aim and position ease at 8/s.
  - On both duel reels the subject now sits 2–16° off centre with a clear line. A shot that showed only walls now shows the airborne killer.
- **Killer or victim view (HL06).** The analyzer records whether the victim
  was looking at the killer (within 45°) when the frag landed, from their
  last drawn view, since they have left the frame that carries their death.
  A victim view needs that, and never comes twice in a row: in a duel
  almost every victim faces their killer.
