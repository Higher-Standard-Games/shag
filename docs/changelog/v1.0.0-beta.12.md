# v1.0.0-beta.12

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

### Bots: measured against people

- `bot:demo-motion` measures how players move and look in recorded matches,
  people and bots by the same code: speed, airtime, distance from walls, view
  turning, flicks and their corrections, hop chains, pitch. `bot:motion
  --keep-demo` keeps its match demo so today's bots are measured the same way.
  Before, bot movement targets were guesses; there was no measure of people.

### Bots: a calmer view out of a fight

- Out of a fight with no enemy in sight, a bot's view eases where it wants to
  look (at most 120°/s, small changes ignored), rests about 10° below level at
  the floor ahead, and glances aside every few seconds instead of leaning
  toward open space all the time. Turnarounds still snap after the reaction
  time. Before, every new wanted view was turned to at once: bots turned
  110–145°/s out of a fight against people's 35, and ran with the view dead
  level where people look 11° down. Measured now: 96–102°/s, 9° down, and
  about a third fewer view reversals.

### Bots: hear as players hear

- Bots hear footsteps when a player's client plays them: on the ground faster
  than 70 u/s and not sliding, where they used to need 250 u/s, so a person
  walking slowly was silent to bots while bots were never silent to people.
  Jumps are heard from 60 u/s up (was 200) out to 720 units (was 800), and
  landings are no longer heard: players hear no landing sound, only the pain
  of a hard one. Ranges come from the game's sound falloff and the volume each
  sound plays at.
