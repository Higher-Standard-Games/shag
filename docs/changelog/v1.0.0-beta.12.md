# v1.0.0-beta.12

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

### Players

- The post-game screen now plays a victory clip on the winner's model, one of
  six picked at random with equal odds: three dances and three taunts, new in
  `washoi_player_animations.pk3`. The rebuilt archive must be published with
  the release.
- After about three kills in ten, the killer's third-person model taunts over
  its upper body while its legs keep running. Bots taunt too. Every viewer and
  demo replay sees the same taunt, chosen from the death event with no network
  change. The next shot, aiming down sights, a weapon switch, swimming,
  climbing, a wall jump or a slide ends it. `animtest once <clip>` plays a
  clip over the current one.
