# v1.0.0-beta.2

**Release Date:** November 3, 2026

## Changes

### Faster WebGPU: the map replays its recorded draws

WebGPU drew the map with as much JavaScript per wall as WebGL2 and then some:
Babylon records each draw as a GPU render bundle, but still selected the mesh,
bound its material, lights and uniforms, and checked the bundle on every frame
before replaying it. On `washoi_dm2` that was a quarter of a frame, and WebGL2
ran the same recorded FFA about a third faster.

Map surfaces that cannot change between frames (frozen materials, fixed
position, no animated shader stage) now skip all of that in the main view: their
recorded bundles go straight into the frame once every buffer they read is
confirmed current. A surface that fails a check, and one surface each frame in
turn, draws the usual way and records a fresh bundle. Water, depth and
reflection passes still see every surface. On recorded FFAs, frames are 21%
faster on `washoi_dm2` (36 surfaces replayed per frame) and 25% faster on
`washoi_dm5` (53), with identical pictures. `perf capture` reports the
replayed surfaces; `?worldbundles=0` in the address turns the replay off for a
session.

### WebGPU keeps textures compressed

The WebGPU device was created without its optional features, so the GPU had
no block-compressed texture formats and every KTX2 texture was decoded to
uncompressed RGBA: on `washoi_dm2`, 231 textures and 30 texture arrays took
four times the memory and sampling bandwidth they take on WebGL2. WebGPU now
asks for BC compression (and ASTC or ETC2 where a phone has them), and those
textures stay BC7 as on WebGL2. The texture arrays, which WebGL2 keeps
uncompressed, are compressed too.

### Less garbage from projectile trails

Each rocket, grenade and plasma trail copied its whole vertex array out of the
mesh and read it back again to measure its bounds, every frame: 46 KB of
garbage a frame in a fight on `washoi_dm2`, about a quarter of everything the
frame allocated. Trails now rewrite a kept array and take their bounds from the
points they write. On both renderers.
