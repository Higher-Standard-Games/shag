## Changes

### Small health bubbles are taken at full health

A +5 health bubble now overheals to 200, like the mega, and decays back to 100
at 1 HP a second. Before, it stopped at 100 and stayed on the map when you
walked over it at full health. Bots go for bubbles at full health as well; one
is left on the map only at 200 HP.
