# v1.0.0-beta.4

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

### Small health bubbles are taken at full health

A +5 health bubble now overheals to 200, like the mega, and decays back to 100
at 1 HP a second. Before, it stopped at 100 and stayed on the map when you
walked over it at full health. Bots go for bubbles at full health as well; one
is left on the map only at 200 HP.

### Fewer frame drops in fights (WebGPU)

Heavy frame drops in a full dm5 duel came from two places. Major garbage
collections paused the game for up to 200 ms: Babylon names every render pass
and command encoder with the frame number, and the browser kept each of those
names until a collection had to walk them all. Those names are now the same
string every frame. And while an explosion was alive, every expiring spark
re-uploaded the explosion buffers whole, sized for 96 explosions, which took
about 7 ms a frame; only the live, changed part is uploaded now. Two smaller
sources of garbage, in the soft-particle depth pass and the world fog of
effects, are gone as well. Over the whole ten-minute demo, frames over 25 ms
fell from 328 to 22 and frames over 50 ms from 11 to none. The measurements
are in `docs/research/webgpu-frame-spikes-2026-11-03.md`.

### Smoother weapon switches (WebGPU)

A weapon switch built the new view model from scratch and threw the old one
away: about 10 ms for the switch and another 45 ms spread over the frames
after it, which showed as a stutter on nearly every switch. The view now keeps
each weapon it has shown, with its barrels and ammo counter, and brings it out
again on the next switch to it: 2.6 ms for the switch, 9 ms over the frames
after it.

Rockets and grenades also stopped interrupting the WebGPU frame replay twice
per shot: their fade-in ended at full opacity, which moved them between draw
queues. They now fade to just short of it. Over the dm5 duel, replay stops
fell from 957 to 771.
