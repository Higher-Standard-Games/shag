# v1.0.3

**Release Date:** Nov 21, 2026

## Changes

- **Home page.** The lobby now opens on **Home**; the Washoi logo at the top
  of the rail leads back to it. Home has three full-height cards: **Quick
  play** (Free For All, over ten match shots that drift slowly and fade from
  one to the next), **Campaign** (the campaign banner's squad and street,
  drifting in its purple stripes) and **Play online** (the four modes in their
  colours). Each card does exactly what its rail key does; the card under the
  pointer widens, lifts and glows. In a narrow window the cards stack. Each
  card's text sits on its colour, which breaks up into the art: pink blocks,
  purple lines, a gold halftone. The lobby's footer now shows the version
  before the downloads indicator.
- **`npm run itch:push`** builds the itch.io builds and pushes them with butler
  (`--staging`, `--production`); the release procedure pushes both with every
  host deploy.
