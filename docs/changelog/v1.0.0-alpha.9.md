# v1.0.0-alpha.9

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

### New map: washoi_dm14 (Reflected)

`wfdm14` from the Warfork map sources is packaged as `washoi_dm14`, served in
`map_washoi_dm14.pk3` with its `tex_washoi_dm14.pk3` texture bank. The BSP is
Jal's build, with 2048 × 2048 lightmaps. It compiles
`textures/factory/pillar03_fx` with `surfaceparm trans`: that additive beam
shader lacked it, so the compiler treated the beams as opaque walls and removed
the faces behind them, leaving the scrolling columns see-through. The served
shader now declares it too. The map's `textures/grates/gratehzh` had no shader
or image in the media set and drew as an untextured panel; it is now the HazelH
grate (`textures/hazelh/grate`) drawn as an alpha-blended material. The 66 textures it introduces are merged into the
`washoi_textures.pk3` master and classified in
`config/map-texture-encoding.json`, so the texture banks can be rebuilt from
`public/data` alone. Washoi DM14 joins the Duel and FFA map pools. Its bots know
47 movement routes learned from the `dm14basics` training demo
(`config/bot-routes/washoi_dm14.json`). The game
manual's map table listed retired pool maps (`wamphi1`, `wbomb2`, `wbomb6`); it
now shows the actual pools.

### New map: washoi_dm7

`wfdm7` from the Warfork map sources is packaged as `washoi_dm7` (Jal's BSP,
2048 × 2048 lightmaps) and joins the FFA map pool. Its new textures
come from Warfork's current media, including the grid and grate materials. Its autumn forest
skybox, `textures/sky/autumtrees` ("FREE - SkyBox Autumn Forest" by Paul,
CC BY 4.0), is added to the texture master.

### Bots aim down sights

Bots raised their sights only with weapons that cannot fire from the hip, so
they sprayed the machinegun, Voltaic, plasmagun and riotgun with their full
hip spread, and fired the Electrobolt with 20° of it. They now aim down sights
whenever it tightens the shot. With the Electrobolt, rocket launcher and grenade
launcher, whose aim takes time to settle, they hold fire until the shot counts
as aimed.

### Spectating

- Watching a player through their eyes on a live server shows their sights and
  zoom when they aim down sights; before, only demos did. Snapshots carry it in
  bit 3 of the movement byte, which older recordings never set.
- The Voltaic beam of the player being watched leaves their gun on screen. Its
  start was placed before the spectator camera moved each frame, so it trailed
  the barrel and jumped around with the view.
- Going to spectator from the Escape menu could put the player straight back
  into play: snapshots sent before the server took the request still held their
  player, and the client read that as a confirmed join.
- Escape opens the match menu while watching a player. Watching someone frees
  the mouse, and the menu opened only when Escape released a captured mouse,
  so it could not be reached until the spectator flew free again. For the same
  reason a click did not move on to the next player; a click on the game view
  now does.

### Terrain rocks drew untextured

`textures/terrain/rocks001_512x512` and `rocks002_512x512` named
`_1024x1024` images the media set did not have, so their surfaces had no
texture. The 1024 × 1024 images are now in the texture master. This affects DM7
and Concrete Strike.

### Additive layers over a material that has its own glow

A shader whose `material` stage named a glow image (its fourth token) dropped
every additive stage after it: the material has one glow slot, already taken,
and the shader still collapsed into a single material. DM14's scrolling pillars
(`textures/factory/pillar03_fx3`) lost their stripes and the pulsing trims
(`textures/factory/trim01_fx`) lost their pulse and scroll. Additive stages that
do not fit the glow slot now draw as their own passes, in script order. No other
shipped shader changes.
