# v1.0.0-beta.12.2

**Release Date:** Nov 14, 2026

## Changes

### Bots

- A bot that keeps asking to move without getting anywhere now turns round,
  whatever it is doing. On the ground, asking to move for 0.6 s while it stays
  within 24 units, slower than 60 u/s and not pressed against an enemy, it is
  blocked. Out of a fight it backs off and plans its route again, away from the
  way it was moving; in a fight it drops the route that led it there, runs the
  most open way for half a second with its aim kept on the enemy, and strafes
  the other way. Before, stall recovery skipped fights and was dropped while an
  enemy was in sight, so a bot could push into a corner until it was killed: on
  dm2 an Insane bot stood 5.4 s in the corner under the 50 health. Insane
  against Insane on dm2, the bot that turns round scored 0.92 frags a match
  more in two runs (13 and 24 matches), and stuck spells of 2 s or more fell
  from 8 to 3.
- A bot knows to the tick when it took an armor or health item itself. No
  event names who takes those, so its own Red Armor was dated half a respawn
  back, and it came back for it at the wrong time.
- Bots check the major items in passing as a player does. Running out of a
  fight, a bot glances at an armor, Mega Health or powerup spot it has not seen
  for 8 seconds when the spot is in sight and within a quarter turn of where it
  runs; a spot in sight is watched every snapshot, and an item found gone is
  dated from when the bot last saw it there, not half a respawn back.
- `bot:ab` arms take `"fightOff": ["unstick"]` and `["itemcheck"]` to measure
  those habits.

### Known

- On dm2 the Red Armor has no route in the bots' navigation graph: it sits in
  an alcove taken from below or beside by the pickup's reach, through its wall,
  or in a jump past it, and bots take it only by chance. Navigation does not yet
  plan a jump that takes an item in passing.
