## Changes

- **Fixed dead jumppads & teleporters on ~12 maps** *(on branch `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — the BSP
  trigger extractor only accepted `target_position` / `misc_teleporter_dest` / `info_notnull` as a
  trigger destination, so any `trigger_push` / `trigger_teleport` aimed at `target_location`
  (wdm12/17/18/19, wbomb6, wda1, wdm11, wtutorial1) or Quake 3's `info_teleport_destination`
  (wbomb2, wctf1, wda4) was silently dropped and did nothing. Surveyed all 38 shipping maps; the
  allow-list now covers every destination classname in use. (wbomb1's pads already resolved via
  `target_position` — verified, unchanged.)
- **Pickup sounds** *(on branch `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — health / armor / ammo / weapon pickups
  now play the Warsow `sounds/items/*` bank for the local player (2D, distance-independent), driven
  by the existing `pickup_notify` event alongside the HUD toast. Armor tiers (shard/green/yellow/red)
  and mega health each get their own sound; also plays in demo playback for the followed player.
- **BSP collision fixes** *(on branch `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — three fixes in
  `SweepCollisionBackend`:
  (1) **Baked `misc_model` decorations no longer collide.** Decorations (wdm2's floor lamps, and also
  the jumppad flames/rings and teleporter models that sat solid on top of the pads) are triangle-soup
  drawSurfs whose shader carries SOLID content. Skipped by shader prefix (`models/`, `mapobjects`).
  World triangle soup **still collides**: q3map2 emits triangle soup for vertex-lit world geometry
  too, and 336 walkable surfaces across 10 maps (wbomb1-6, wctf2, wdm1, wdm17, wtutorial1) are held
  up by nothing else — dropping all soup collision put holes straight through walkways, 30 of them
  bottomless. Measurements in `docs/research/bsp-triangle-soup-collision.md`.
  (2) **Detail brushes (`CONTENTS_DETAIL`) are solid again.** `shouldCollideWith`/`ContentFlags`
  treated detail as non-solid, but detail is a vis/portal flag — Q3 collides detail brushes. This had
  made SOLID|DETAIL geometry walk-through (wbomb1: 462, wdm12: 199 brushes — glass panels/railings).
  (3) **Face collision used a symmetric player hull.** `traceTriangleSwept` offset the box from the
  surface by `max(|mins|, |maxs|)` per axis, so on a floor it used the head half-height (40) instead
  of the foot half-height (24) — resting players and bots **exactly 16 units above every patch and
  triangle-soup surface**. Now uses the same asymmetric support point `traceBrush` always did, so
  curved/vertex-lit floors and brush floors agree. Measured across the pack: 65 spawn/item points
  settle 2–16u lower, none higher, none lose support.
- **Fixed remote players/bots rendering ~97 units off their real position** *(on branch
  `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — bodies appeared to warp around the map as they turned, and to hover off
  the floor. The simulation was never wrong (a headless yaw sweep with zero movement input shows
  0.0000u drift); this was purely the render transform. The Mixamo clips carry heavy root motion on
  `mixamorig:Hips` — at `MODEL_SCALE` 36 that is 82u forward for run, 39–51u sideways for the strafes,
  246u for slide. `RemotePlayerRenderer` parents the model to an anchor it rotates to the player's
  yaw, so that displacement sits in the anchor's LOCAL space and the yaw **rotates** it: the body
  traced a circle around the spot it should have been standing on, and which direction it flew
  depended on which locomotion clip was playing. `PlayerModelManager.resetRootMotion()` was written to
  cancel exactly this, but walked `getChildTransformNodes(true)` — direct descendants only.
  vanguard.glb nests as `__root__ > RootNode > mixamorig:Hips`, so the walk only ever saw `RootNode`
  and **the Hips branch was dead code**. Now walks the full subtree (only `RootNode` and
  `mixamorig:Hips` match those names anywhere in the 70-node tree, so it stays targeted) and restores
  the Hips **bind pose** rather than only zeroing X/Z — which also kills the vertical bob that floated
  the body. The same direct-descendants-only traversal was fixed in the two clone-time cleanup passes
  that clear `.animations` / stop animatables. Headless repro: 97.5u offset swinging through a full
  turn before, 0.4u after. Regression tests in `src/engine/__tests__/PlayerModelRootMotion.test.ts`.
- **Teleporter in-going effect** *(on branch `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — entering a teleporter read as
  just vanishing (the entry ghost sits behind the camera post-teleport, and there was no teleport
  sound at all). Added the Warsow `sounds/world/tele_in.ogg` entry cue (2D for yourself, 3D at the
  entry for remote players) plus an inward "implosion" speed-line burst at the entry — the mirror of
  the outward exit burst — so a teleport now reads as being pulled in. Preloaded on map load.
- **Machinegun reload** *(on branch `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — replaced the old near-free 40-round /
  0.13s "reload" (a cosmetic hitch) with a real magazine: **30-round clip + 120 reserve + 1.5s reload**.
- **Removed the gunblade weapon (full clean sweep)** *(on branch `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — it was
  vestigial (fired once then dry, in no gametype loadout, spawned by no map; Blade is the proven
  default). Deleted the weapon def, the `GUNBLADE_BLAST` projectile def, and the `GUNBLADE_AMMO` def,
  plus every reference: table/registry entries, bot profile, `isDefaultWeapon` checks, HUD
  slot/colour/label entries, fire + impact sounds, damage-type case, the `defId==='gunblade'` /
  `case 'gunblade'` demo back-compat hooks, the `weapon_gunblade`/`ammo_gunblade` pickup mappings, and
  the `gunblade_blast` model preload. Blade's charged `blade_blast` is fully independent and simply
  reuses the gunblade blast **art files** (model/impact/sound media in the pk3) — those paths are
  unchanged (`TODO: custom blade art`). Old gunblade-era demos no longer render their blast
  projectiles (accepted). Tests updated (MG clip fixture, weapon/alias coverage lists).
- **Voltaic beam reload** *(on branch `0.9.21-qa-improvements`)* — the continuous beam used a 60-cell
  clip that emptied in ~2.9s then stalled for a 1.3s reload (~31% downtime). Restructured to a
  120-cell clip + 60 reserve + 0.5s reload — same 180-cell total, but the beam now runs ~5.8s
  between brief reloads (effective DPS 100 → 134). See `docs/research/weapon-values-audit.md` for the
  full actual-timings/DPS table and the remaining flagged balance items (plasmagun DPS, machinegun
  clip, vestigial gunblade). Weapon timing/reload comments corrected to match the tick-quantized
  reality; documented that `reloadTime` is read only by the `clip` reload model.

## Technical Notes

- `TriggerTable.DEST_CLASSNAMES` unified into one shared allow-list (both trigger types accept the
  same point-entity classes, matching the engine's classname-agnostic `G_PickTarget`). Regression
  tests added from the real wdm12 (`target_location`) and Q3 (`info_teleport_destination`) records.
- New `client/FPSPickupAudio.ts` mirrors `FPSCombatAudio`: label→sound classifier + `preloadFPSPickupAudio()`
  hooked into the map-load predecode (live + demo). No protocol change — the sound is chosen from the
  existing `pickup_notify` label, so small/large health tiers collapse to one bubble sound.
- Cleanup: the three audio banks' predecode calls collapsed into one `client/FPSAudio.ts#preloadFPSAudio()`
  aggregator (was the same `Promise.all([...])` repeated at each map-load site); unified the two
  near-identical `TriggerTable.DEST_CLASSNAMES` lists into one shared allow-list.
  (`SweepCollisionBackend.buildMeshFace()` was briefly deleted as dead code and is back — world
  triangle soup still needs it; see the collision entry above.)
- Verified (no change): enemy footsteps/jumps already emit as 3D positional events — the server sends
  per-player `velocity`+`grounded` for all players, and `FPSMovementAudio` plays remote movement via
  `playSfx3D`. Audibility (remote volume/attenuation) is a separate tuning knob, left as-is.

## Validation

- **Suite**: 166 files / 1618 tests passing; `tsc --noEmit` clean; biome clean on all touched files
  (the one remaining warning in `PlayerModelManager.ts` is a pre-existing `as any` material cast).
- **Collision, measured over all 36 shipping map pk3s** (headless probes against the real
  `SweepCollisionBackend`, not fixtures):
  - Walkable solid triangle-soup faces with no support underneath: **0** — same as before the cycle.
    The intermediate "drop all soup collision" build measured 336 across 10 maps, 31 of them
    bottomless; that is what the shader-prefix rule replaced.
  - Decoration soup correctly skipped and world soup correctly kept, spot-checked per map
    (wdm2 3 skipped / 131 kept, wbomb4 54 / 4447, wtutorial1 128 / 641) — skipped shaders are
    lights, air-conditioners, jumppad flames/rings, teleporter shells.
  - Drop-probe from every spawn + item entity origin in the pack (1733 points) vs the pre-cycle
    baseline: **0 lost their ground, 0 raised**, 65 settle 2–16u lower (the removed hull float).
- **Triggers**: replayed the old vs new `DEST_CLASSNAMES` resolution across all 38 maps —
  **0 existing destinations changed**, 31 previously-dropped triggers now resolve. Each of the 31 was
  eyeballed: matching targetnames, plausible arc heights above their pad, no location-marker
  `message` keys.
- **Movement determinism**: headless yaw sweep (full 360°, zero movement input, settled on ground)
  drifts **0.0000u** — confirms the warping reports were render-side, not simulation.
- **Root motion**: headless repro of the render transform measured **97.5u** of body offset swinging
  through a full turn before the fix, **0.4u** after.
