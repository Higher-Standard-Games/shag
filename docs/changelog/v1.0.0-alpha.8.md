# v1.0.0-alpha.8

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

- Weapons: the Electrobolt carries the sleeker angled sight of the Lasergun,
  Plasma Gun and Rocket Launcher instead of the Machinegun's boxy one. Its holo
  pane, LED strip and ammo counter sit on the new sight. The new model ships in an updated
  `washoi_weapons.pk3`.

- Weapons: the Plasma Gun's chambers glow green. Their plasma core, halo and
  glass were drawn as opaque painted surfaces, so the dark glass hid the core.
  The chambers now turn slowly while idle, spin up while firing, and wind down
  over 1.5 s after the last shot, instead of turning at one speed.

- Weapons: other players' Plasma Gun and Grenade Launcher show their barrels.
  Only the main gun model was loaded for a gun in another player's hand
  (including the winner showcase), so the Plasma Gun had no chambers and the
  launcher no drum.

- Weapons: the Grenade Launcher's drum turns through the whole 800 ms between
  shots, a full revolution and one chamber, instead of snapping one chamber
  in 0.16 s.

- Weapons: grenades fly 20% faster (1080 instead of 900) and an aimed lob
  reaches 30% farther: gravity on grenades is about 930 u/s² instead of 840,
  and the fuse lasts 1.625 s instead of 1.5.

- Weapons: the Voltaic's barrel channel shows its teal energy tube again. The
  tube, its glow and an emit strip had no bone weights in the model, so the
  game drew them collapsed to a point and the channel looked empty and black.
  They are now bound to the gun like its other parts, in an updated
  `washoi_weapons.pk3`.

- Console: `map <name>` works for every player, not only with `developer 1`.
  On washoi.net it starts a practice server of your own: one of the lobby's
  maps, no bots, nobody else, nothing rated. Before, the console refused it,
  and the lobby only took the request from its own machine. One practice
  server per player at a time, at most three started a minute.

- Weapons: the Electrobolt's teal barrel glow shines out of the gun as a
  gradient, as in Warsow, instead of drawing as a solid teal slab traced by the
  weapon outline. Glow gradients now add their light over the gun, and the
  outline leaves them out. Pickups, dropped weapons and other players' guns
  draw the glow the same way; they showed its black as a black slab.

- Weapons: the Voltaic's beam shows the moment you fire from the hip. It
  appeared only after a 0.25 s delay, while its shots were already hitting.

- Weapons: the Machinegun's bolt cycles back with each shot and is racked once
  during the reload. The bolt model shipped with the gun but was never shown.

- Weapons: the Electrobolt's rail stays attached to your barrel for its whole
  flash. It was left where the muzzle was when the shot landed, so from the
  hip, with the gun kicking and turning, its gun end hung off in the air.

- Weapons: a dropped weapon flies and lands where the server has it. The
  client threw it with its own physics: it could come to rest in one place
  and then slide to the server's spot a moment later, and on some floors,
  such as beside the Voltaic on washoi_dm11, it fell through. The client now
  replays the server's own throw over the same map collision, spinning in
  the air and coming to rest on its side.
