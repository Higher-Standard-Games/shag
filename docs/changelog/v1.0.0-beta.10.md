# v1.0.0-beta.10

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

### Manual accuracy

- Corrected engine setup and packaging instructions to use browser application
  selection through `SHAG_APP` or the game package commands instead of `GAME`.
  Clarified that manifest generation also writes the PK3 lookup index.
- Aligned the game manual's Node requirement with the pinned Node 24 runtime
  and its certificate instructions with the active WebSocket transport.
- Updated Amphi and S&D map guidance to the configured Washoi pools, documented
  revisioned map packages, corrected Duel/FFA countdowns to five seconds, and
  distinguished loadout modes and Amphi respawns from Duel and S&D. Clarified
  that S&D excludes map weapons and Quad and resets pickups each round.

- Bots come out of a teleporter facing its exit, as players do. They kept the
  view they went in with and turned round only after their reaction time, so
  a bot leaving a reversing teleporter looked backwards and missed what was
  in front of it.

- Bots turn back and forth far less: in 4-bot FFA on washoi_dm1 and dm2 their
  view swings away and straight back about 60% less often than before.
  - Lining up a learned trick jump, a bot looks the way the trick starts while
    it shuffles onto the mark. It used to turn to face the mark each time it
    stepped past it, spinning on the spot.
  - A bot chasing an enemy keeps chasing that one until another is clearly the
    better lead, rather than turning between enemies whose trails cross.
  - A bot forgets an enemy who was fragged, instead of chasing where they would
    have run on to.
  - While chasing an enemy just lost from sight, a bot keeps looking where
    they went, as a player watches a corner, instead of turning along its
    route and back whenever they reappear.
  - A bot gives up for a few seconds on an item its route cannot reach, such
    as a weapon dropped down a ledge. It used to walk at it into the wall and
    turn back to the route over and over.
  - A drop with a blocked landing is avoided from every ledge above it, not
    only the one the bot tried, so it no longer tries each neighbour in turn.

- Bots on their way to an item they need keep going when they hear an enemy
  further than a corner away; they still ready a weapon for them. They used to
  turn to chase every sound, give up as it faded a second later and turn back.
  Once chasing, a bot keeps chasing rather than dropping in and out as the
  enemy's distance crosses that limit.
- A bot that takes the second-best item by mistake keeps to it for its
  commitment, as intended. The next decision used to take the better item back,
  so the mistake showed only as a turn away and back.
- Lining up a learned trick jump, a bot creeps onto its mark crouched, lets
  itself coast to a stop and stands up before starting. It used to shuffle
  back and forth across the mark for seconds and often give up. It no longer
  starts a trick from the floor below or above the mark.
- A bot gives up for a few seconds on an item it has stayed beside for a second
  without taking, such as a weapon dropped on a lip above it, instead of
  circling it at full speed.
- Steering round a wall ahead, a bot keeps turning the side it chose for a
  moment instead of choosing again from each new heading.
- Bots crouchslide down any straight, open stretch of their route. They used to
  slide only when the next route waypoint was 320 units away, which a straight
  corridor with closely spaced waypoints never offered. A waypoint a bot slides
  or runs past is left behind instead of being turned back to.
- On the way to an item, a bot first takes another worth having that costs
  little detour, such as a health pack just off its route to the Mega, and
  keeps to that plan rather than dropping it as the distances change. Which
  item it is going for is unchanged. In 4-bot FFA on washoi_dm2 the bots picked
  up about 40% more health and armor a minute; on dm1, where the items lie
  further apart, about the same.

- Bots shoot when their view is on the enemy, not from the moment they decide
  to. A bot that noticed someone behind it fired all the way round its turn.
- A missed jump or failed trick on the way to an item no longer makes a bot
  give up on the item: it avoids that jump and takes another route. Each miss
  put the item itself on cooldown, so a bot that failed a hop on the floor by
  washoi_dm2's Red Armor walked away from it. A jump missed again and again is
  avoided for longer each time, up to 40 s.
- After a slow gun's shot, a bot switches to the other gun it carries while
  the first reloads, when that one suits the distance and can fire well
  before: an electrobolt followed by the machinegun or riotgun. Rolled once a
  shot at prediction-skill odds.
- Running out of a fight, a bot leans its view toward open space near its
  route instead of the wall a corner points it at. In 4-bot FFA on washoi_dm2
  the time it spends looking at a wall within 192 units fell from about 37% to
  32%, with view turning slightly lower.

- Bot routes keep further off walls: route waypoints are placed up to 64 units
  out from the wall they were sampled against, and a route along a wall costs
  more than one down the middle of the room. In 4-bot FFA the time bots run
  within 24 units of a wall fell by 2–3 points on washoi_dm1 and dm2. Bot nav
  caches rebuild once.

- Bots run each walking stretch of a route along one line planned through it
  (down the middle of rooms, round corners on the outside) instead of from
  node to node, and wall avoidance stands aside while they do. About 40% of
  the walk links in a map's bot graph were walks that bend round a doorway or
  drop off a ledge, which bots ran straight at, into the wall; those now keep
  the corners of the walk. A straight walk up a ledge higher than a step, which
  the old check passed, is no longer a walk. In 4-bot FFA the share of running
  time within 24 units of a wall fell from 19-22% to 11% on washoi_dm1 and
  from 17-20% to 9-12% on dm2, and wall avoidance turns the bots on 2-4% of
  their running time instead of a quarter of it. Bot nav caches rebuild once.
- `npm --prefix src/games/fps run bot:routes` checks the bots' route lines
  offline, for every route from a spawn point to an item, in seconds.

- `npm --prefix src/games/fps run bot:motion` measures how bots move in a
  headless match: how often their view swings away and back, how fast it
  turns, time spent still (by brain state and per bot), sliding and airborne,
  health and armor picked up, how far the view sees out of a fight, how close
  bots run to walls and how fast, and deaths per minute. Watching demos was the only way to judge bot movement
  before. Its match demo goes to a temporary folder, not `public/data/demos`.

- The credits dialog's Close and the video export page's buttons are the menu's raised keys again. The welcome guide's restyle in beta.9.1 had left them as plain browser buttons and made the credits card as large as the guide.
