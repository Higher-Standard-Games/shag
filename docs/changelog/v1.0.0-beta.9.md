# v1.0.0-beta.9

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

- Draw the armor shard, green, yellow and red armor and the megahealth from packaged models (`washoi_models.pk3`) instead of their generated sculptures, which are kept in the recipe list, commented out. The models float, bob and turn like classic Quake items: all turn together once every 4.096 seconds and each bobs on its own phase, lifted clear of the floor. The blue core of the megahealth glows: an emissive mask built from the strongly blue parts of its texture, bright enough for bloom.
