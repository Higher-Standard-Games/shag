# v1.0.0-beta.12.1

**Release Date:** Nov 14, 2026

## Changes

- Full-match video export has the **Background music** picker of a highlight reel. The chosen song plays from its **Start from** position; when it ends, the game's own playlist follows as it does in a match, its other songs in a shuffled order that comes round again with no gap, until the video ends. The music fades out at the end card, and game sounds play under it at the highlight reel's levels. A full match had game audio only.
- Each song of a full match's music is decoded only while the 30-second audio window being mixed needs it, so a twenty-minute match holds one or two songs in memory, not the whole playlist.
