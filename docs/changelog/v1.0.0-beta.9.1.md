# v1.0.0-beta.9.1

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

- Third-person weapons no longer go missing from demos and highlight videos. When a player died or left, their gun was put away twice, once from their hand and once more when their body was released; two players could then be handed the same gun, which was drawn in only one of their hands, most visibly the Plasma Gun in FFA highlights.
- Loading a map a second time no longer fails with "GPU preparation timed out: final map entities". The weapon symbols on ammo boxes kept a texture that went away with the previous map, so the next load waited on them until it gave up. Highlight video export loads the map twice and failed on every map with ammo, as did playing a demo after another one.
