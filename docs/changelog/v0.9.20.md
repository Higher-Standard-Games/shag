# v0.9.20

**Release Date:** 2026-07-23

## Summary

Focus areas this cycle:

1. **Voltaic/Lasergun beam graphics** — replace the beam VFX with a procedural GPU renderer and put the authoritative damage-ray endpoint on the wire (design: `docs/research/VOLTAIC_DESIGN.md`).
2. **Electrobolt rail + weapon VFX** — procedural rail for the bolt weapons, and procedural muzzle flashes for every weapon (design: `docs/research/ELECTROBOLT*`).
3. **Procedural health & armor pickups** — runtime-generated pickup visuals replacing the MD3 models (design: `docs/research/PROCEDURAL_ITEMS.md`).
4. **QA / assorted-bug follow-through** — the v0.9.19 QA carry-overs plus a batch of assorted bug fixes (hitscan occlusion, post-match polish; status in `docs/research/v0.9.19-QA.md`).
5. **Map-learning bot foundation** — team-safe perception, BSP-derived navigation/cache, deterministic traversal and movement sequences, and reproducible analysis tooling (design and checkpoint: `docs/research/BOT_IMPROVEMENT.md`).
6. **Babylon.js 9 renderer modernization** — Babylon 9.17, clustered dynamic lights, maintained loader APIs, default-on PK3-aware KTX2 sidecars, and an experimental WebGPU renderer behind an explicit URL opt-in while pipeline parity is completed (plan/status: `docs/research/BabylonJS-9-upgrade.md`).
7. **Babylon Audio V2 FPS soundscape** — PK3-indexed Warsow weapon/world sounds, spatial combat and movement, Bobot voice feedback, hit confirmation, and the kill echo.

## Changes

- **Voltaic/Lasergun beam graphics** *(merged to main)* — new `src/engine/weapon/voltaic/` procedural GPU beam renderer (analytic single-quad shader + thin-instanced remote/chain batch) replaces the old 18-cylinder re-jittered bolt. The server emits a `voltaic_beam` event (exact damage ray, contact point, surface normal, impact kind) so the electrical shape draws client-side over a spine that matches the real damage ray — no more through-wall / max-range remote beams or endpoints jumping at the 20 Hz fire cadence. Legacy `lasergun` is collapsed into `voltaic` everywhere (canonical IDs, classname + ammo aliases, pickups/registration/bot profiles). Beam wander retuned to a slow deterministic meander (was ~7 Hz spin between shots). Pixel-exact muzzle from the animated `tag_flash` socket. fps `ac64cf3`, engine `f74e198`.

- **Electrobolt rail** *(merged to main)* — the electrobolt/instagun bolt is now a procedural analytic-helix GPU rail (cold blue-white core, twin helix, muzzle crack + terminal nucleus), driven by an authoritative `electro_rail` wire event (exact endpoint) for both local and remote shooters, replacing the old re-jittered bolt. Anchored to the animated gun muzzle for the local shooter (was the eye origin → a near-camera smear). fps `516f04b`/`4a0860c`, engine `ae1f7b2`/`953c1c4`.

- **Procedural health & armor pickups** *(merged to main)* — health and armor map pickups are now runtime-generated "modern sci-fi" sculptures instead of MD3 models, with **no** change to gameplay, the server-authoritative active mask, respawn timing, pickup ids, or the map/network identity — only the client visual is replaced. New engine `PickupVisual` seam (`PickupOptions.visualFactory`; the model path becomes a fallback) plus an FPS `world/pickups/procedural/` module: a recipe-driven family covering all 9 tiers, a scene-scoped geometry/material cache (clone-per-pickup shared geometry), a fail-closed active-state machine (first mask applies silently; active→inactive plays a detached collection burst that survives the persistent visual being hidden; inactive→active assembles), and an `r_pickupProcedural` toggle (default on) that falls back to the MD3s. Health = a bright scrolling-gradient energy core + cross emblem (25 HP yellow, 50 HP orange→yellow) with a slow pulse; armor = a technical shield of dark hex plates with glowing energy rims, clustered by tier. fps `d7a7347`, engine `534eb48`.

- **Procedural muzzle flashes** *(merged to main)* — a shared procedural muzzle-flash renderer draws a flash for every weapon (GLB weapons shipped no `_flash` model, so only the light flickered before), with per-weapon styles (burst / fiery plume / electric arcs) that ride the animated barrel socket. Beam weapons keep their own corona. fps side is presentation-only. engine `83eb51e`/`57c3778`/`c32b6af`.

- **Bot map learning and authored movement** *(merged to main)* — headless bots now build and persist BSP-fingerprinted, schema-versioned navigation graphs instead of relying on map-specific coordinates. Typed A* steps retain stable pickup IDs and exact teleporter/jumppad metadata; bounded 125 Hz rollouts validate ordinary jumps with the authoritative movement model; and `npm run bot:analyze` produces reproducible map reports. A deterministic sequence director executes and verifies ordinary jumps, route crouchslides, conservative strafe jumps, teleporter/jumppad traversal, and final-edge slides into active items, then cools failed edges and replans. Team relations are isolated per bot and a final line-of-fire gate suppresses direct friendly fire across state-machine and hybrid decisions. This is the completed Phase 0–3 foundation checkpoint; richer surface discovery, advanced jumps, combat choreography, gametype directives, and lifelong learning remain planned. engine `bd025aa`/`8116dd9`/`12f6adb`, fps `fe9b0d3`/`c24d4eb`/`15fef36`.

- **Hitscan world occlusion** *(merged to main)* — `traceHitscanRay` now traces the BSP world and rejects targets occluded by geometry (bots were shooting through floors) and picks the nearest target, so a body shields whoever is behind it. Regression tests added; server determinism unaffected. fps `1e7cb62`.

- **Post-match & assorted-bug polish** *(merged to main)* — endgame music now plays at match end in **all** modes (was network-host-only) via a host-agnostic playlist trigger; player gameplay input (movement + blade/fire) is frozen on the end screen while chat + leave-lobby stay live; and the winner showcase gets a dedicated diorama camera framing a centered, straight-on hero shot relative to the model (not the death spot). Also: viewmodel no longer renders black under dynamic lights (#2), on-gun ammo counter restyle (#3), 'VIEW DEMO' plays in-engine instead of downloading (#6), and dropped weapons no longer despawn the tick they drop. fps `a952217`/`af97b1b`/`13fc28f`.

- **Babylon.js 9.17 renderer modernization** *(merged to main)* — migrated Babylon core/loaders to 9.17, replaced soft-deprecated `SceneLoader` class calls and remaining core barrel imports, clustered dynamic gameplay lights, and completed a default-on PK3-aware KTX2 pipeline with 3,020 generated/validated sidecars plus local decoder workers. Map pickups now spawn only after scene/material finalization and map reset owns their generations; composite weapon pickups preserve attachments. `WebGPUEngine` starts with bundled local GLSLang/TWGSL and selects WGSL for Q3 NodeMaterials, but real lobby testing exposed invalid WebGPU shader/pipeline layouts and white frames, so WebGL2 is again the default. Use `?renderer=webgpu` only for isolated A/B work until the visual/pipeline gate passes. The KTX1 compatibility audit also fixed backend-independent upload of 56 alpha-only legacy textures.

- **Babylon 9 lighting POCs & renderer tooling** *(merged to main)* — two tested WebGL2 lighting POCs land on the Babylon 9 base: **textured area lights** (`RectAreaLight` + emission texture, vendored LTC lookup data, `arealight` console command) turn any image into a physically-emitting panel; and **volumetric god rays** (`VolumetricLightScatteringPostProcess` in sun-tracking and fixed-anchor modes, `GodRaysController`, `godrays` console command, opt-in per BSP map via a worldspawn `godrays` key) add sun shafts / light shafts carved by world geometry. Also a **renderer performance-capture** tool (`perf` command / `RendererPerformanceCapture`) records backend, map, KTX2 state, CPU/GPU frame timing, draw calls, shader-compile time, active meshes, and WebGPU bundle counters through Babylon's public `EngineInstrumentation`/`SceneInstrumentation`, replacing private stat access. The **WebGPU re-test** confirmed a hard 12-UBO/vertex-stage adapter ceiling (a pipeline needs 14) that config can't lift, so WebGPU stays opt-in and postponed behind the WS7 native-WGSL shader port.

- **Static BSP world freeze** *(merged to main)* — static BSP world geometry (`bsp_world` + its `bsp_tex_*` texture groups) now freezes its world matrix and bounding-info sync at map build, skipping the per-frame transform/bounds recompute Babylon does for geometry that never moves; autosprites are excluded (they billboard by rewriting vertex data each frame). A pure CPU win that scales with map complexity, gated by `r_bsp_freeze` (default on, applies on next map load) so it can be A/B'd against a baseline with `perf capture`.

- **GPU precipitation** *(merged to main)* — rain and snow now use Babylon 9's `GPUParticleSystem` on WebGL2, moving simulation as well as rendering off the CPU through transform feedback. The existing box emission, camera following, wind, intensity, color and snow/rain profiles are preserved; `emitRateControl` retains the former emission semantics. A CPU implementation remains only as an automatic compatibility fallback when transform feedback is unavailable. Rapid stop/restart now retires the exact old particle system instead of a delayed callback accidentally disposing the replacement.

- **Babylon Audio V2 + FPS soundscape** *(merged to main)* — short sound effects now run through a minimal Babylon Audio V2 engine service: one volume-controlled SFX bus, async browser unlock, PK3-index package loading, shared decoded buffers, listener attachment, and bounded per-location sources. The FPS submodule owns the Warsow sound policy and wires accepted weapon fire, projectile explosions, attacker-only `hit_0..3` confirmation, the oversized `kill.wav` frag echo, and Bobot pain/death/jump voices to authoritative snapshot events. Footsteps are driven by actual replicated travel distance instead of a timer, are 3D-positioned for other players, and select normal/metal/silent banks from BSP `METALSTEPS` and `NOSTEPS` flags. Demo playback uses the same event functions and resets cadence on seeks; existing streaming playlist/music playback remains deliberately separate and simple.

- **God rays as a map feature** *(merged to main)* — the volumetric god-ray POC is now map-driven via `GodRaysController.loadFromWorldspawn`, activated at BSP map-load by priority (master-gated by `r_godrays`, default on): (1) a **Warsow/Q3 sun entity** — a targeted `sun`/`_sun` light authored in the map (common on the recent `wbomb1‑6`) drives shafts along the map's real sun; (2) a worldspawn `godrays "1"` key with optional look overrides; (3) a mapper-placed `light_volumetric` point emitter (one active — VLS is a single full-screen pass). The look is a live-tunable, persisted `r_godrays_*` cvar family (`exposure`/`decay`/`weight`/`density` apply to the active pass in real time, `samples` on next activation), so it can be dialed in from the console. Entity parsing lives in a pure, unit-tested `MapGodRayEntities` module (Quake→Babylon coordinate + sun-direction conventions locked); presentation-only, WebGL2, no gameplay/determinism impact.

- **Textured area lights as a map feature** *(merged to main)* — the area-light POC is now map-driven: `AreaLightManager.loadFromMap` reads `light_area` BSP entities at map-load (next to the sky/god-ray hooks) and builds `RectAreaLight` panels — a glowing sign/screen that physically lights the room. Keys: `origin`, `angle` (yaw; -1 up, -2 down) or `_arealight_dir`, `_size`, `_color`, `light` (intensity), optional `_arealight_image`. Because area lights aren't clustered — each consumes a real per-surface light slot, and BSP world is texture-grouped so mesh/range scoping can't localize them — the only safe budget lever is count: `r_arealights` (master, default on) + `r_arealights_max` (default 4) gate/cap it. Parsing lives in a pure, unit-tested `MapAreaLightEntities` module. Presentation-only, WebGL2.

- **Area-light auto-placement from emissive surfaces** *(merged to main)* — opt-in `r_arealights_auto` (default off) auto-places area lights on a map's brightest **fullbright** world surfaces (the Q3 flag lamps/signs use), so glowing signs actually light the room on existing maps without hand-placed entities. The pure, unit-tested `MapEmissiveSurfaces` module reads the raw BSP world faces (no mesh-builder changes), derives each surface's Babylon centroid/normal/size, culls specks below a min area, and ranks by area so the largest signs fill the `r_arealights_max` budget (after any explicit `light_area` entities). Each auto-placed panel projects the sign's **own loaded BSP texture** as the emitter (reused from its `bsp_tex_*` material — no re-load), so the cast light carries the sign's colors/shape, not flat white. Each panel's brightness scales with the shader's authored surface-light strength — the parser now captures `q3map_surfacelight` (Q3) / `lightingIntensity` (Warsow), so a surface a mapper marked as a strong emitter casts more light than a dim one. Area-light specular is low by default (glossy metal no longer mirror-streaks) and `r_arealights_intensity` dials overall brightness. Default-off pending further browser tuning of placement quality (facing, size heuristic, which surfaces).

- **Emissive glow layer** *(merged to main)* — a scene-wide Babylon `GlowLayer` (`GlowEffect`) adds a controllable emissive glow on top of the existing HDR bloom: neon strips, signs, energy surfaces and weapon FX read as glowing at a chosen strength even when they aren't bright enough to trip the bloom threshold. Driven by the persisted `r_glow` cvar (**default 0 / off** — bloom already covers bright surfaces, so glow is opt-in polish; tunes live). Lazy like SSR: the `GlowLayer` runs its own blur passes every frame, so it is created only when `r_glow > 0` and disposed live when set back to 0 — "off" is zero-cost, not zero-intensity. Presentation-only, WebGL2.

- **Screen-space reflections (SSR), v1** *(merged to main)* — opt-in dynamic reflections on glossy surfaces via Babylon 9's `SSRRenderingPipeline` (`SSREffect`): the metal floors/walls reflect the *live* scene instead of the static skybox cubemap. Material-driven — only surfaces whose reflectivity clears a threshold reflect (the existing gloss maps decide, no per-surface tagging), with the map's skybox cubemap as the off-screen fallback. GPU-expensive, so **off by default** (`r_ssr`, `r_ssr_strength` tunes live) and GPU-tier-tuned (ray-march `step`/`maxSteps` scale with tier); fails gracefully if unsupported. Subtle "wet sheen" defaults, not a mirror. Design + water follow-up (Warsow reflective-shader key vs a bespoke water shader) in `docs/research/BABYLON9_SSR.md`. Presentation-only, WebGL2.

- **Advanced graphics in Settings + tuned defaults** *(merged to main)* — Bloom, Glow and Reflections (SSR) are now exposed under Settings → Graphics (persisted, apply live, reset with the tab) alongside the existing controls. Defaults set for a clean competitive baseline: `r_glow 0` (off), `r_ssr 0` (off), `r_bloom 1`; each is opt-in polish rather than on-by-default. Mouse look default lowered `m_sensitivity 2 → 1`.

- **Scene-scoped effects rebind on scene rebuild** *(merged to main)* — `GlowEffect` and `SSREffect` are now registered engine **scene services** (beside `PostProcessingManager`) instead of being initialized once at startup, so they reset + re-init against the current scene on every rebuild. Closes a stale-scene hazard (WO13): SSR would otherwise rebuild a GPU pipeline against a **disposed** scene on camera swaps — a real hitch/leak source — and glow/SSR would silently stop working after a scene reload until a full page refresh.

- **Autosprite billboards follow the eye again (Babylon 9 fix)** *(merged to main)* — BSP autosprites (light coronas/glow strips; `deformVertexes autosprite`/`autosprite2`) stopped tracking the player under 9.17 and froze at a fixed angle. Gameplay is multi-camera (`scene.activeCameras = [eye, weapon]`) and 9.17 leaves `scene.activeCamera` pointing at the last-rendered **weapon** camera — which is parented to the eye, so its `.position` is a fixed local ~0. The per-frame billboard update now uses the eye camera's **world** position (`activeCameras[0].globalPosition`), falling back to the single camera in freefly/spectator. The billboard math itself was unchanged from 8.56; only the camera it was fed was wrong. Independent of `r_bsp_freeze`.

## Technical Notes

- **Wire**: `voltaic_beam` (event id 16) added to `BinaryProtocol` + the `SnapshotEvent` union; encode/decode are byte-symmetric and the exhaustive client dispatch enforces the handler at compile time.
- **Determinism preserved**: the beam event is presentation-only output (no sim-state feedback); the wander retune is a pure function of tick+shooterId. `server:tick` checksum unchanged (`2768943778`).
- **Renderer split**: beam state moved out of the 1,200-line `HitscanEffects` into `voltaic/` (`VoltaicShaderMaterial`, `VoltaicBeamGeometry`, `VoltaicEffectRenderer`, profiles). Straight invariant damage core; all electrical motion is an analytic GPU field driven by the server/demo clock (frame-rate independent, prewarmed). Local truth reconciliation (`isVoltaicTruthAligned`) keeps the spine on the real damage ray.
- **Wire (rail)**: `electro_rail` added to `BinaryProtocol` + the `SnapshotEvent` union (byte-symmetric codec, exhaustive client dispatch), carrying the authoritative rail endpoint so both local and remote rails draw over the real damage ray.
- **Pickup visual seam**: `PickupVisual` / `PickupOptions.visualFactory` in the engine `Pickup` decouple the rendered representation from the authoritative lifecycle (position, ground placement, proximity, server active-mask, respawn, disposal). Procedural health/armor own their meshes + private materials + collection transients; shared prototype geometry and the structural material are scene-cached and disposed on scene teardown. No `PickupTable` / `PickupSystem` / snapshot changes.
- **Muzzle/rail motion**: procedural muzzle flashes and the rail are presentation-only (no sim feedback); the muzzle rides the projected `tag_flash`/barrel socket each frame.
- **Bot map cache**: immutable graph data is stored under `.cache/bots/maps` and invalidated by BSP hash, schema, trigger resolver, and movement-profile fingerprint. Mutable perception, enemy memory, failed-edge cooldowns, and item state remain scoped per bot or match.
- **Bot sequence safety**: sequence timing uses authoritative replicated ticks and verifies physical state transitions before declaring success. `HybridBrain` cannot overwrite an active authored sequence, and all decision paths pass through the same ally line-of-fire gate.
- **Renderer fallback contract**: WebGPU support detection and initialization are isolated behind a factory; unsupported adapters, compiler startup errors, and device-init failures return the established WebGL2 engine. Babylon engine observables now drive resource revalidation for both WebGL context restoration and WebGPU device restoration.
- **Headless asset parity**: the CLI now reads `public/engine.json` at its composition root and injects the same `GamePaths` configuration used by browser startup. Its default game-data path is the canonical `public/data`, while DOM-only world indicators stay dormant under `NullEngine`.
