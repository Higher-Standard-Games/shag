# v1.0.0-beta.13

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

### Search & Destroy bots

- Bots play the bomb as a team. One bot goes onto a placed or armed bomb and
  two teammates guard it, each between the bomb and one way in; the rest fight
  for the ground round it. Before, every defender walked onto the bomb past
  whoever was shooting them and stood there. The bot on the bomb fights what
  it sees while the clock leaves time to finish, and a defuser waits beside
  the bomb until no attacker has been seen near it for two seconds.
- Guarding bots never stand still: they run about their ground watching the
  way in, and a fight does not pull a guard off it. Out of a fight the blade
  goes away for a gun.
- Bots call the team's plan in team chat ("Going A", "2 on A, 3 on B",
  "Arming A, cover me", "Retake B, go!", "Defusing, cover me"), and a person
  can order the team: `go`, `wait`, `retake`, `stack a`.
- Strafe jumps chain off each landing and accelerate with forward and a side
  key, as people do; `bot:demo-motion` reports how far bot movement is from a
  person's.
- Bots steer their feet clear of walls, corners and ledges in every state,
  fights included, instead of only on some navigation: a sweep of the body
  ahead and a fan of rays lean them off walls and round corners. In S&D they
  run within 8 units of a wall about half as often. Before a climb or a wall
  jump they run the movement forward to check it works from where they are.
- On the go, the bot paired with the bomb carrier goes into the site first
  and the carrier follows two seconds behind. Strafe-jump chains take the
  side away from a wall beside them, and bend off a wall coming up.

### Campaign

- A story campaign of eight operations against bots, from **Campaign** in the
  lobby's menu. The operations run down the page, each with its whole comic
  card and its details beside it, on a track that shows how far you have got:
  cleared (with your most frags in a win), next (with **Play**), or classified. The page opens at your next one.
  Across the top the squad stands on their street, fading into the page's
  own deep purple; scrolling the operations drifts the street and the squad
  at different speeds, and an idle handheld-camera sway slides the street
  gently behind them. The
  first four are written: Amphi against the Cartel King on `washoi_amphi2`,
  free for all against four rioters on `washoi_dm1`, S&D with a crew of four
  on `washoi_snd1`, and a duel with the Glitch Queen on `washoi_dm2`.
- **Play** shows the operation's comic card on a black screen, one
  framed panel at a time with the rest blurred, moving on after five seconds or
  at a click or key. Behind it the lobby opens a private room with the
  operation's map and bots; the match loads when the card is done.
- A campaign match draws you as the robot and your opponents in the
  operation's model, whatever the model settings say.
- Winning clears the operation and unlocks the next. The lobby keeps progress
  per account in `campaign.db` beside `players.db`, from the game server's
  signed result; the browser keeps a copy. Missions are defined in
  `config/campaign.json`. Campaign rooms are built for co-op: everyone in one
  must have unlocked its mission.
- A campaign match has no warmup or ready-up: once you are in, a 10-second
  countdown ("get ready", three, two, one) holds everyone at their spawn, then
  the match goes live.
- A campaign match is closed to everyone but its own players: its game server
  (`--campaign <mission>`) turns away spectators and the lobby never offers a
  drop-in seat.
- Demo format v8 records the campaign mission in the header. Campaign demos
  stay out of **Latest**, the featured picks and the recent demos; each player
  finds theirs under **Your demos**. The reader still plays v1–v7.
- The lobby notes when an account first finishes the whole campaign
  (`completedAt` in `campaign:state`), for rewards to come.
- Concrete Strike (S&D on `washoi_snd1`) is a sabotage run: your side attacks
  every round with no swap at the half, and the first side to 10 rounds wins,
  10-9 included (`"side": "attack"` in `config/campaign.json`, passed to the
  server as `--campaign-side attack`). The defenders are police; your crew is a
  mix of cartel and riotkitty, set per bot (`model` on a campaign bot).
- In a campaign match teammates are always drawn green (the Lime colour,
  tinted and outlined), whatever the colour, tint and outline settings say.

### Lobby

- Quick play no longer readies you up. It opens the room, adds the four bots
  and votes a map, then waits for you to press **Ready**, so you can look
  around the menu first without the match loading.
