# v1.0.0-beta.3

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Changes

### WebGPU whole-frame snapshots (experimental, `?snapshot=1`)

On WebGPU, `?snapshot=1` in the page address records a frame's render passes
once and replays them on the frames that follow. While the scene's draw list
holds, a replayed frame skips Babylon's mesh selection and material binding.
Before each replayed frame the controller writes what changes in place:
moved meshes, skeletons, effect batch counts, outline and shader-material
matrices, and light uniforms. On the dm2 FFA demo, 87% of frames replay, and
frame rate is ×1.5 against WebGPU without snapshots. The design and
measurements are in `docs/research/webgpu-snapshot-plan-2026-11-03.md`.

The first build froze players, projectiles and beams in replayed frames. Their
per-draw matrices, light directions, and the collapse of just-hidden meshes
were written where the recorded draws did not read them. These now update
every replayed frame.

Material animation stood still in replayed frames as well: explosions, sparks,
beams and water stopped at their recorded time, scrolling Quake stages
stopped, and fades, pickup pulses and the weapon glow held. The controller now
learns which materials change from frame to frame and binds those again in
replayed frames, at about 4% of a replayed frame's time.

Snapshots stay opt-in until every pool map has been checked.
