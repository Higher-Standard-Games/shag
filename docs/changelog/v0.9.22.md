# v0.9.22

**Release Date:** unreleased — living changelog

## Summary

Focus areas this cycle:

1. **Bots in Search & Destroy** — the one thing v0.9.21 shipped broken: attacking bots hold at their spawn instead of moving on the site. The directive path checks out end to end, so the next step is data rather than reading, starting with the nav graph's node count on the bomb maps. Quad awareness is the other bot gap — they have no desire term for the most valuable item on the map.
2. **Interactive server consoles** — `bot:server` and `net:server` are firehoses with no filter, search or live introspection, which is why the bot bug above is still open. Reuse the client console's `CommandRegistry` for log filtering, a searchable ring buffer, and live actions (`navgraph`, `directives <bot>`).
3. **Unified water** — the follow-up carried from v0.9.20 and now researched in `docs/research/WATER.md`: BSP liquid classification, a quality-scalable material family, deterministic swimming in the shared movement profile, and underwater presentation. `acidwdm2` is the test map.
4. **Search & Destroy tuning against humans** — no S&D round has been played human-vs-human. The 10 s freeze, the 90 s round, the score limit, the two-round lead and the fallback site radius are all unmeasured.
